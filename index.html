Good to know

Questions, answered.

A few details about using Velora to work with your images.

Is Velora free to use?

Yes. This version runs in your browser and does not require an account or a paid image-processing API.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No image upload is performed by this page. The selected image is read and processed locally in your browser. Your browser, extensions, or hosting environment are separate from the image-processing code.

Which formats can I export?

You can export JPEG, PNG or WebP. Animated GIFs are not preserved as animations by canvas-based processing; GIF or BMP inputs may need to be exported as a supported format.

Why can a converted image be larger?

File size depends on the image content, output format and quality setting. PNG is lossless, while JPEG and WebP quality settings can reduce size with some visual trade-offs. Not every conversion makes a file smaller.

Can I use Velora on my phone?

Yes. The interface adapts to mobile screens. Very large images may take longer or need more memory, depending on your device and browser.
